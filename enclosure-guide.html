Enclosure Design Guide

If the enclosure is not constructed to the recommended dimensions, light from the Aquafire LED system may escape the enclosure and illuminate the ceiling. To help prevent this, incorporate a light trap into the enclosure design. Refer to the chart below for recommended finished dimensions.
