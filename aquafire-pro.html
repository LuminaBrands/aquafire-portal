Key Features

30+ Flame Colors — Select from over 30 preset color options via the included remote or the dedicated iOS/Android app.
Dual LED Light Bars — Enhanced flame realism with wider light angles for a natural, immersive look.
No Venting Required — No chimney, no gas line. Install virtually anywhere with just a standard outlet.
Direct Plumbing Ready — Connect to a water line for continuous operation without manual refills.
Home Automation — Compatible with smart home systems for seamless integration and voice control.
Gang Units Together — Combine multiple inserts for up to 40 ft of continuous flame on a single control unit.
