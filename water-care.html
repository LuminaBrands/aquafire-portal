Softener Guide

Choose the right Vapor Pure system for your installation.

Usage Type Water Hardness Recommended System Capacity
Recreational (3–4 days/wk, ≤6 hrs/day) Soft to Hard (≤180 PPM) Residential Grade 300 grains
Recreational Very Hard (>180 PPM) Commercial Grade 700 grains
Commercial (daily, >6 hrs/day) Any hardness level Commercial Grade 700 grains

Cartridges are interchangeable between Residential and Commercial systems. If you find you’re replacing more often than expected, switch to a Commercial Replacement Cartridge on your next refill.
