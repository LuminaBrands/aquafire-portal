Body
Markdown-lite: ##/### headings, **bold**, *italic*, `code`, [text](url), ![alt](url) images, - and 1. lists, | pipe | tables |, and > note: / > caution: callouts. Everything else is escaped — raw HTML will not render. “Insert image” uploads to Firebase Storage (help-media/, needs docs/storage-rules.md published) and writes the markdown for you — “by URL” skips the upload and asks for a link instead.

Before you publish

  • Warm, plain second-person voice, US English — same as the rest of the portal.
  • No prices or MSRP. Link the store page instead (https://www.aquafire.com/…, opens in a new tab).
  • Cutout clearance is nominal + ⅝″ in total, split across the outside edges — never “per side”, never the spec sheet’s + ¼″. Prefer linking enclosure-guide.html over restating numbers.
  • Never name the chat assistant in customer copy — internally it’s Ember, to customers it is just “the chat”.
  • Every fact traces to docs/source-material/ or an already-published portal page. No invented specs, phone numbers or URLs.
  • Portal links are relative (troubleshoot.html); other help articles are help.html?article=<slug>.
  • Fractions with the inch mark: ⅝″, 14⅛″.
