        Flames uneven?

        Flames non-existent?

        Flames not as tall as they normally are?


        If you have ever turned on your Aquafire or Aquafire Pro and the flames don't look quite right,
        then this article is for you!

        The common culprit for uneven or low mist is high water levels in the mist tanks. First and
        foremost, your insert needs to be cleaned multiple times a year and run regularly to turn over
        fresh water. Please follow our Preventative Maintenance Guide to keep your insert running
        optimally! If preventative maintenance didn't solve the issue or your insert is within the first 90
        days, then high water is usually caused by a stuck float sensor or a mist maker that needs to be
        cleaned or replaced.
