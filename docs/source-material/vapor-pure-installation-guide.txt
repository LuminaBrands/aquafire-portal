             PLUMBING CONSIDERATIONS

       Connect to a cold water supply pipe only.
       Shut off cold water supply pipe (see Diagram 1).
       Review Diagrams 1, 2, and 3 and install cartridge instructions.
       Note flow of water “arrow” on system (see Diagram 1).
       Determine placement of system and flow arrow direction before
       attaching bracket to head.
       Horseshoe clips must be used on all tubing connections of
       system – failure to do so voids warranty.
       Fittings provided with system must be used.
       Use teflon tape only on fittings with threads. Use of anything else
       voids warranty.
       Note the Flow Arrow, Inlet (I), and Outlet (O) fittings on the Head
       (See Diagram 1).
      WARNING: DO NOT USE WITH WATER THAT IS MICROBIOLOGICALLY
      UNSAFE OR OF UNKNOWN QUALITY WITHOUT ADEQUATE DISINFECTION
      BEFORE OR AFTER THE UNIT.

    FLOW DIRECTION OF WATER (see Diagram 1):

     1. Water flows from the Cold Water Supply pipe to the Inlet Shutoff
       to the Inlet fitting of Head.
     2. Water then flows from the Outlet fitting of Head to the Aquafire
       unit or filtered-water dispenser.

    MOUNTING OF COMPONENTS & CONNECTING THE TUBING:

     1. make sure the Cold Water Shutoff and the Lever of the Shutoff
       to the Aquafire unit will be easy to reach when the system is
       mounted.

             LOCATION CONSIDERATIONS



       Total space needed for complete installation of the AF300:
                             13” H x 6” W x 5” D


       Total space needed for complete installation of the AF700:
                              21” H x 6” W x 5” D




    The location selected for the system components should:


       Allow space to mount Head. Mount Components only in a
       vertical/upright position as shown in the Diagram 1.
       Provide a solid mount for the Head. Head can be installed under
       the kitchen sink. However, it can also be installed on a
       beam/wall in the basement.
       For AF300: Allow a minimum clearance of 9” under the Head so
       that the cartridge can be lowered for easy removal.
       For AF700: Allow a minimum clearance of 17” under the Head so
       that the cartridge can be lowered for easy removal.
       Allow space on both sides of the Head for the water
       connections.




       Check with local plumbing codes and consult your
           licensed plumber for proper installation.

                              INSTALLATION

      Attach Bracket to Head using 4 short 1/2” screws provided


       1. Using the Bracket as template, mark and then screw in 2
      long 1” screws to mount Head in a position to conveniently
      attach the tubing from the Cold Water Supply pipe to the Inlet
      of Head, then the tubing from the Outlet of Head to the
      Aquafire unit or filtered-water dispenser. (See Diagram 1). Allow
      a minimum clearance of 9" for AF300 and 17" for AF700 under
      the Head so that the cartridge can be lowered for easy
      removal.


       2. Mount Head and tighten screws


       3. Turn off Cold Water Shutoff. (See Diagram 1)


       4. Locate collet, see Diagram 3.


       5. Install Aquafire Pro unit or Aquafire with Direct Plumb Kit
      (Sold separately) using the instructions included with it, but do
      not run tubing to it at this time. NOTE: Aquafire unit may
      already be installed.


       6. Ensure Cold Water Supply is suitable to connect to a 1/4"
      OD water tube. May require a reducer to be installed (not
      included).

                                      INSTALLATION

           7. SECTIONS OF TUBING SHOULD BE A MINIMUM OF 2” IN LENGTH. Cut a
          length of tubing long enough to connect the Cold Water Supply pipe
          attachment close to where the Head & Cartridge is to be mounted. Cut
          tubing square. (See Diagram 1 and Diagram 3).


           8. Using instructions that originally came with the pipe attachment,
          connect one end of the tubing to the Cold Water Supply pipe
          attachment (See Diagram 1 and Diagram 3).


           9. Ensure the outside diameter of tubing is free of score marks. Insert
          tubing into collet. There will be some resistance when tube is initially
          inserted. Make sure tubing is inserted all the way into collet until it
          meets the tube stop, a full 3/4”. Tube may be gripped but not sealed if
          not fully inserted. After insertion gently pull on the tubing so that the
          collet around the tubing comes just far enough away from the body of
          the Connector fitting to be able to slide a horseshoe locking clip
          between the collet and the Connector body. (See Diagram 3)



           10. Cut a length of tubing long enough to connect the Outlet
          fitting (O) of Head to the Shutoff of the Aquafire unit or Direct
          Plumb Kit.


           11. Connect one end of the tubing to the Outlet fitting (O) of
          Head and the other end to the Shutoff of the Aquafire unit or the
          Direct Plumb Kit, if applicable. (See Diagram 1 and Diagram 3)

               INSTALLING CARTRIDGE


    Align Unlock Icon on cartridge label with the Inlet Fitting of Head
    (I) and insert cartridge.
    Grasp Head firmly with one hand and with other hand push
    cartridge up into Head.
    Continue grasping Head firmly with one hand and with other
    hand turn cartridge to the right until it stops, about 1/4 of a turn.
    Make sure Lever on the Aquafire Shutoff valve has been turned
    clockwise until it stops in off position. (See Diagram 1 and
    Diagram 2)
    Slowly turn lever on Cold Water Supply counterclockwise
    just enough to have water start flowing.

    Cartridge will now fill with water and begin to flow to the
    Aquafire Shutoff Valve. Check for leaks.
    Slowly turn the lever on Aquafire Shutoff valve counterclockwise
    until it stops in ON position. Lever of Shutoff Valve will be straight
    in line with the body of Shutoff (See Diagram 2). Water will flow
    to the Aquafire unit.
    Turn the lever of the Cold Water Supply counterclockwise until it
    stops in the ON position. Check for leaks for the next 24 hrs.




          Note:
          Filtered water may appear cloudy for 1 – 3 days
          as the cartridges are purged of air. You can
          check to make sure it is only air bubbles causing
          the cloudiness by filling a large glass with water
          and letting it stand. The bubbles will rise and the
          water will become clear. The water is fine to use.
          If the cloudiness persists, please contact your
8         local dealer.
            USING HARDNESS TEST STRIPS


    Test Strips are Individually Foil Wrapped

    Access Aquafire water tanks:
      1. Lift the Aquafire lid to access water tanks.
     2. Unscrew and remove the top tank cap labeled
        “WATER” to test the water in the top tank.


    Test the New Cartridge:
      1. Follow the instructions on the foil wrapper for
        performing the test.
     2. Match color on strip with color chart on the foil
        wrapper. Note result.


    When to Replace Cartridge
        HARDNESS: Replace cartridge when the color of
        the Total Hardness strip appears to be any shade
        different than 50, or at least annually.
        IRON: Replace cartridge when the color of the Iron
        strip appears to be any shade different than 0,
        or at least annually.
