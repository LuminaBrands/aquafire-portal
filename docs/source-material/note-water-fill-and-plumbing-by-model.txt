Water fill & direct-plumb capability by model
=============================================

Source: Lumina Brands (Stefan), Jul 2026 — provided directly for the
knowledge base. This is the authoritative statement of how each model
connects to water; it supersedes conflicting older copy, including the
2026 buying-guide blog's "the Pro and Original offer an optional Direct
Plumb Kit" (the Pro needs no kit) and the earlier note here that only the
Lite 40"/60" can't direct-plumb (no Lite size can).

AQUAFIRE PRO
  - Comes with the ability to connect directly to a water line out of the
    box — no add-on needed.
  - Can also be manually filled using the integrated water pump port
    located on the top right corner of the burner.
  - Comes with a dispensing pump, allowing the unit to be emptied at the
    press of a button.

AQUAFIRE ORIGINAL
  - Does not come with the ability to connect directly to a water line out
    of the box.
  - The Direct Plumb Kit add-on enables a direct water-line connection —
    easy install, sold and shipped separately.
  - Can also be manually filled using the integrated water pump port
    located on the top right corner of the burner.

AQUAFIRE LITE / GATSBY
  - Does not come with the ability to connect directly to a water line out
    of the box, and does not currently have the option to upgrade.
  - The units are manually filled using the integrated water pump port
    located on the top right corner of the burner.
