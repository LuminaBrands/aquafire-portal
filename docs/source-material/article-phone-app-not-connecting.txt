        Congratulations on installing your new Aquafire Pro! If you choose to operate your Aquafire Pro
        via the AFIREWATER Prestige App, there are a couple details to consider before you connect:

            The Aquafire Pro will only connect to a 2.4 Ghz router network. 4G and 5G home wifi
            networks generally have the ability to create a 3G network that runs on 2.4 Ghz. These water
            vapor fireplace inserts are sold all over the world and not all locations have the USA's faster
            networks so our international manufacturer still uses a 3G card.

            Once you have the correct 2.4 Ghz router network, the instructions for downloading and
            connecting to the app need to be followed precisely. Customers who download the app and
            try to connect out of sequence have more problems than those that follow the step-by-step
            guidelines in the Owner's Manual (it can be found on page 9 and 10) and/or YouTube video:
