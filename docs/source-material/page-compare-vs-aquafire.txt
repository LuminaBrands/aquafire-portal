TAB 2 — AQUAFIRE vs. OTHER WATER VAPOR (three side-by-side demo videos;
competitors shown generically, not named)

  "Alternative Brand Competitor" (left column):
    - Flat flames lack depth and realism
    - Stringy, finger-like mist plumes
    - Limited to 6 set colors
    - No water softener — mineral buildup over time
    - Foot-mounted: harder install, never sits flush

  Aquafire (center, hero):
    - Dual Flame Spectrum(TM): volumetric, full-depth flames with vibrant
      color mixing
    - Dual Air Vapor Flow System(TM): moves like real wood fire — natural,
      irregular flame motion
    - Modular Ambiance: gang multiple burners for one seamless,
      edge-to-edge flame
    - UVC Disinfection and Vapor Pure(TM): softened, UVC-sanitized water —
      no buildup, no upkeep surprises
    - Side-Mount Design: flush, recessed finish with an easier install

  "Generic Budget Unit" (right column):
    - Looks like smoke, not fire
    - Can't be ganged — visible gaps between units
    - Dense mist causes moisture and corrosion
    - Cheap internals mean frequent maintenance
    - Lacks North American-based product support

  Verdict: "Only one water vapor fireplace technology looks like real
  fire — and it's the only one built to last."

UV-C — THE POWER OF UV DISINFECTION
-----------------------------------

Our mission is to provide you with an experience that is both safe and
environmentally sustainable, while ensuring that cleanliness and hygiene
remain a top priority. That's why every Aquafire is equipped with
state-of-the-art germicidal UV-C lamps which disinfect the vapor as it
flows out of the unit and into the air. This powerful technology
eliminates up to 99.9% of viruses, bacteria, spores, microorganisms, and
airborne pathogens, keeping your environment clean and healthy.
