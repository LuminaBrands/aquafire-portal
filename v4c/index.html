2 For new owners
Day one is mostly water and patience. Distilled or softened water, a first fill, and you’re minutes from first flame.

Your first day, in order

  1. Unbox and place — or plan the enclosure first if you’re building one.
  2. Check your water — hard water shortens mist-maker life. Look yours up by ZIP.
  3. First fill & first flame — the quick start walks each model through it.
  4. Register your warranty — two minutes, worth it.
