Why is my flame low?

Usually one of three things. In order of likelihood:

  1. Mist maker needs cleaning — mineral buildup dims the mist. It’s a 10-minute job, due every 3 months.
  2. Water level — too low or too high both weaken the flame.
  3. Sponge filter — a worn filter makes the flame thin or foggy.
