Important Safety Information
  • Electrical: Indoor use only. Ensure proper grounding — do not use extension cords.
  • Water: Always maintain the water level. Use ClearShield or Vapor Pure to treat hard water.
  • Installation: Follow all clearance requirements. Enclosure must use non-combustible materials only.
  • Supervision: Keep children and pets away from the mist output area during operation.

5 Key Features

Classic Orange Flame — Warm, natural amber-orange flame effect that captures the timeless look of a traditional fire.
Single LED Light Bar — Illuminates the water mist with even, warm light for an authentic flame presentation.
No Venting Required — No chimney, no gas line. Install virtually anywhere with just a standard outlet.
Direct Plumbing Ready — Connect to a water line for continuous operation without manual refills.
Home Automation — Dry contact terminal for integration with smart home systems and external switches.
Gang Units Together — Combine multiple inserts for up to 40 ft of continuous flame on a single control unit.
